# Roadmap

Status key: **Done** means implemented, tested, and verified on `main`; **Experimental** means working research implementation with reproducible benchmarks; **Next** is planned future work. The complete master directive is preserved in [`MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md`](MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md).

## Completed Foundations & Milestones (`v0.1.0-alpha.1` → `v0.4.0`)

- **Done — Foundation & MIT License:** Public repository on `main`, Rust 2024 workspace (13 safe-Rust crates), `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, zero Python dependencies, Windows + Ubuntu CI, `cargo-deny` zero-ignored-advisories policy, MIT License (`LICENSE`), automated public GitHub Releases with SHA-256 checksums, and 17-topic source-backed prior-art review.
- **Done — Milestone I (Q-Scope Foundation):** Bounded & constant-memory streaming Safetensors & GGUF v2/v3 payload readers, exact bit-level `F16`/`BF16`/`FP8`/`F32`/`F64`/`Q8_0`/`Q4_0`/`Q4_1`/int codecs, sharded index & `config.json` parsers, one-sided Jacobi exact thin SVD + HMT randomized SVD, and `qml-scope` observatory with cross-layer Grassmannian subspace affinity.
- **Done — Milestone II (Quantization Baselines):** Reproducible `FP16`, `BF16`, `INT8_SYM`, `INT8_ASYM`, `Q4_0`-style `INT4_SYM`, `Q4_1`-style `INT4_ASYM`, `LowRankSvd`, and `SparseResidual` implementations with exact byte-for-byte `StorageBreakdown` accounting and Rayon-parallelized direct compressed `LinearOperator::matvec`.
- **Done (Experimental) — Milestone III (RMQ & Two-Pass Streaming MAP):** RMQ Shared-Basis (`U_shared · C_l · V_sharedᵀ + R_l` via 2D-HOOI with RPCA-inspired outlier peeling), RMQ Shared-Core (`A_l · S · B_l + R_l`), affinity-gated RMQ (`RmqAffinityGated`), falsifiable Gaussian control ablations, and constant-memory two-pass streaming MAP allocator.
- **Done (Experimental) — Milestone IV (Out-of-Core QMF v1 & Atomic Q-Forge):** Multi-GB QMF v1 binary container (`QmfFileReader` lazy out-of-core reader, `write_to_file_atomic` with source-output alias protection, 64 KiB streaming CRC-32 + SHA-256 verification, `Arc` shared-latent deduplication, `RmqSharedCore`, on-demand `decode_row`) and cooperatively cancellable `qml-forge` pipeline.
- **Done (Experimental) — Project Acceleration (`v0.2.0-alpha.1`):** Persistent GPU-resident compressed `QCoreBackend::GpuHybrid` across 6 WGSL compute kernels (`FP32`, `FP16`, `BF16`, `INT8_SYM`, `Q4_0`, `Q4_1`) with reusable scratch buffers and real `SmolLM2-360M-Instruct` benchmarks (`exp-003`).
- **Done (Experimental) — Q-Behavior & Scalable Q-Genesis (`v0.2.0-alpha.2`):** 13th crate `qml-behavior` (hidden-state tracing, cosine/L2 drift, RSA, strictly runtime-only reversible causal interventions — discovering `SmolLM2-360M` Layer 31 quantization cliff), role/depth-stratified `qml-genesis`, user profiles, and direct Genome-to-QMF v1 export.
- **Done — PROJECT PRISM (`v0.3.0`):** Public native multi-pass WGPU 3D scientific observatory in `qml-desktop` featuring 7 custom WGSL shaders, `Rgba16Float` HDR selective bloom, ACES filmic tone mapping, interactive Orbit/Ortho cameras, 3D ray picking across 7 3D workspaces, 5 persisted graphics quality presets, custom vector iconography, and visually verified `2560×1440` / `1920×1080` native GPU screenshots (`157–187 FPS` on RTX 4070).
- **Done (Experimental) — PROJECT RESONANCE & EVENT HORIZON Wave I (`v0.4.0`):**
  - Complete-artifact Sub-3-Bit Research Ladder (`QMR-300` through `QMR-150`, `3.00 -> 1.50` exact complete on-disk BPP).
  - Blockwise Randomized Fast Walsh–Hadamard incoherence processing (`−37.9%` / `−39.5%` KL and `137.5×` / `209.4×` lower perplexity inflation on matched-byte `INT2` controls with zero extra bytes).
  - Closed-form least-squares affine refit + Gaussian sigma-clipping for `INT3_ASYM` (`8 codes/3 B`), `INT2_ASYM` (`4 codes/B`), and packed base-3 `TERNARY` (`5 trits/B`, Lloyd–Max); 1/2-stage RVQ (`VectorQuantMatrix`) with zero-multiply LUT matvec; **PROJECT ECHO** compact INT8 low-rank + sparse recovery adapters.
  - Variance-additive scale-invariant **MAP-B** (`BehaviorImportanceMap`), held-out 4-category **Capability Retention Suite**, **Q-Genesis Extreme** sub-3-bit genes, **9 persistent GPU-resident WGSL compute kernels** (adding `INT3_ASYM`, `INT2_ASYM`, and `TERNARY`), Consumer VRAM Fit Matrix (`1.7B–32B`), and full real-model evaluation (`exp-004` on `SmolLM2-135M` and `SmolLM2-360M`, including Layer-31 selective protection study).
