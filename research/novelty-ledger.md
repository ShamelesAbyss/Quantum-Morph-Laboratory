# Research novelty ledger

This ledger records proposal status and measured experimental outcomes; it is **not** a claim of patentability or world-first novelty. A 17-topic first prior-art batch is documented in [`prior-art-index.md`](prior-art-index.md) and [`prior-art-synthesis.md`](prior-art-synthesis.md).

## Change protocol

For every proposal, add dated source-backed comparisons, rejected formulations, experiment identifiers, and evidence labels. Keep established literature, QML hypotheses, independently reproduced results, and QML contributions distinct. Do not use "novel," "world-first," "patentable," or performance-superiority language without compelling evidence and review. Preserve failed hypotheses.
