# Roadmap

Status key: **Done** means implemented, tested, and verified on `main`; **Experimental** means working research implementation with reproducible benchmarks; **Next** is planned future work. The complete master directive is preserved in [`MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md`](MASTER_DEVELOPMENT_DIRECTIVE.md).

## Completed Foundations & Milestones (`v0.1.0-alpha.1` → `v0.5.0`)

- **Done — Foundation & MIT License:** Public repository on `main`, Rust 2024 workspace (13 safe-Rust crates), `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, zero Python dependencies, Windows + Ubuntu CI, `cargo-deny` zero-ignored-advisories policy, MIT License (`LICENSE`), automated public GitHub Releases with SHA-256 checksums, and 17-topic source-backed prior-art review.
- **Done — Milestones I–IV (Q-Scope, Baselines, RMQ, MAP, Out-of-Core QMF v1 & Atomic Q-Forge):** Streaming Safetensors & GGUF v2/v3 readers, exact bit-level codecs, one-sided Jacobi & HMT SVD, Grassmannian cross-layer affinity, conventional baselines, outlier-peeled RMQ Shared-Basis/Core, affinity-gated RMQ, constant-memory two-pass MAP, out-of-core `QmfFileReader`, and atomic Q-Forge with cooperative cancellation.
- **Done — PROJECT ACCELERATION, Q-Behavior & PROJECT PRISM (`v0.2.0-alpha.1` → `v0.3.0`):** Persistent GPU-resident compressed inference (`GpuHybrid`), `qml-behavior` mechanistic tracing/RSA/reversible causal ablations, stratified `qml-genesis` with QMF export, and the **PROJECT PRISM** native multi-pass WGPU 3D scientific observatory (`7` WGSL shaders, `Rgba16Float` HDR bloom, interactive 3D picking across 7 workspaces, `157–187 FPS`).
- **Done (Experimental) — PROJECT RESONANCE & EVENT HORIZON Wave I (`v0.4.0`):** Complete-artifact Sub-3-Bit Ladder (`QMR-300..150`), blockwise Randomized FWHT incoherence (`−37.9%` / `−39.5%` KL, `137.5×` / `209.4×` lower PPLx at zero extra bytes), LS-refitted `INT3`/`INT2`/base-3 `TERNARY`, LUT RVQ, PROJECT ECHO, variance-additive `MAP-B`, 4-category Capability Retention Suite, 9 persistent GPU WGSL matvec kernels, Consumer VRAM Fit Matrix, and `exp-004` Stage A benchmarks.
- **Done (Experimental) — PROJECT EVENT HORIZON Wave II (`v0.5.0`):**
  - **Activation-Aware Blockwise Cholesky GPTQ + Hadamard (`qml-quant::gptq`):** Streaming `64×64` block-diagonal `XᵀX` Hessian collection, exact orthogonal covariance rotation `Q H_b Qᵀ`, salience-ordered Cholesky error compensation, adaptive sample shrinkage damping, activation-weighted LS grid refitting, and activation-weighted ECHO recovery with zero extra storage bytes.
  - **Full GPU-Resident Single-Submission WGSL Transformer (`qml-hardware::gpu_full` + `QCoreBackend::GpuFull`):** 6 new WGSL shaders (`RMSNorm`, bit-exact GPU `Hadamard FWHT`, `RoPE + KV-Append`, online-softmax `GQA Attention`, `SwiGLU`, `VecAdd`) delivering **`22.1×` real end-to-end speedup** (`1.84 -> 40.70 tok/s`, up to `63.3 tok/s` at 2-bit) over `GpuHybrid` on RTX 4070.
  - **Multi-Architecture & Multi-Shard Scaling (`qml-model`, `qml-forge`, `qml-runtime`):** Qwen2 attention biases (`q/k/v_proj.bias`), unified single + multi-shard `SafetensorsSource`, zero-duplicate-RAM `forge_path_to_file_atomic`, `KvCacheMode::Int8`, `ReferenceSuiteCache`, and exact **GGUF K-quant** decoding (`Q2_K`, `Q3_K`, `Q4_K`, `Q5_K`, `Q6_K`).
  - **Progressive Stage A → Stage B → Stage C Evidence (`exp-005`):** Benchmarked `SmolLM2-360M`, `SmolLM2-1.7B`, `Qwen2.5-1.5B`, and **2-shard `Qwen2.5-3B` (`6.17 GB` BF16 -> `829.6 MiB` VRAM at `2.253 bpp`, `PPLx = 2.76×`, `55.4%` Top-1, `18.6 tok/s`; `PPLx = 1.01×` at `4.50 bpp`; `PPLx = 1.07×` at `3.25 bpp`)** live on RTX 4070.

## Next Milestones (PROJECT EVENT HORIZON Wave III & Beyond)

- **Next — Stage D (`7B–8B`), Stage E (`12B–14B`) & Stage F (`27B–32B`) on 8 GB Consumer GPUs:** Add resumable layer-checkpointed disk streaming in Q-Forge, quantized VRAM INT8 KV-cache & layer-streaming CPU/GPU hybrid placement inside `GpuFull`, and benchmark `7B` (`Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` / `Llama-3.1-8B`), `14B`, and `27B–32B` models live in `<= 8 GB` VRAM.
- **Next — Breaking the Sub-2-Bit (`1.75–1.50 bpp`) Ternary / Multi-Codebook Barrier:** Add GPU-accelerated 2-stage RVQ / AQLM lookup-table kernels, full-dimension / 128-block Hessian GPTQ with PV-Tuning-style coordinate refinement, and Mistral / Gemma / MoE architecture support.
- **Next — Hierarchical Activation-Calibrated Q-Genesis & Expanded Capability Benchmarks:** Connect `qml-genesis` directly to cached activation-calibrated perplexity/KL fitness, resumable populations, and expanded long-context & coding/math evaluation benchmarks.
