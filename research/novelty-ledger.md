# Research novelty ledger

This ledger records proposal status; it is not a claim of novelty. Entries remain **unreviewed** until primary sources and reputable implementations have been compared.

| Proposal | Current description | Status | Required next comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relational Morph Quantization (RMQ) | Explore representing layer weights through shared latent structures, layer-specific transforms, and residual information (including the proposed form `W_l ≈ A_l × S × B_l + R_l`). | Untested hypothesis; no novelty claim | Compare to shared-factorization, tensor/low-rank methods, codebooks, and residual quantization; count all overhead and test direct operations. |
| Morphological Adaptive Precision (MAP) | Explore precision allocation using measured functional importance and cross-layer error propagation. | Untested hypothesis; no novelty claim | Compare to mixed-precision, sensitivity-guided quantization, activation-aware methods, and rate-distortion optimization. |
| Quantum-inspired cognitive projects | Speculative explorations of bounded latent states, interference-like signed/complex representations, structured dependencies, and adaptive computation. | Speculative; not implemented | Define precise classical math, complexity bounds, conventional baselines, and falsifiable success criteria before implementation. |

## Change protocol

For every proposal, add dated source-backed comparisons, rejected formulations, experiment identifiers, and evidence labels. Do not use “novel,” “world-first,” “patentable,” or performance-superiority language without compelling evidence and review. Preserve failed hypotheses.
